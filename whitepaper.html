01. Executive Summary

Trade N Build [$TNB] is the primary utility token for Raytown Construction and the economic heartbeat of the FastTrack Community Eco-system, primarily centralized around the Raytown hub. Built on the Base Network, it provides a high-speed, low-cost economic layer for digital property owners and metaverse architects.

Zero-Fee Philosophy: There are NO monthly subscription fees to use any of our products. The ecosystem is fueled purely by community asset ownership and active participation.

02. Tokenomics & Liquidity

The $TNB token is engineered for sustainability, deep liquidity, and a guaranteed floor value.

  • Total Supply: 1,000,000,000 (1 Billion) $TNB
  • Floor Price: 0.000001 ETH (approx. $0.00245)
  • Network: Base (Layer 2)
💧 Uniswap V4 & The 40% Growth Engine

$TNB is paired with BaseETH in a Uniswap V4 liquidity position. To ensure deep liquidity and defend the price floor, 40% of all USD earnings from Raytown Construction and FastTrack services are automatically converted to BaseETH and injected into the LP. This provides continuous buy-back pressure!

04. Asset-Backed Reward Distributions

Holding specific ecosystem assets transforms them into revenue-sharing instruments!

🛒 Metaventure Sales Share

Holders of assets from the FastTrack, Raytown Construction, and Raytown Rebelz collections earn 1% of the total monthly sales price from any FastTrack Community Metaventure.

🏁 Speedway Property Yields

Owning property on an active FastTrack Speedway generates $TNB based on the track's total monthly income (race entry fees, shop sales, etc.).

  • Empty Plot: 1% of track income (Paid in $TNB)
  • Plot with Structure: 3% of track income (Paid in $TNB)

Idle Track Safety Net: If a track has no activity in a given month, owners still earn 0.05% of the previous month's total earnings!

05. The "Build-to-Earn" Loop & Utility

$TNB is both a reward and a currency within the ecosystem:

  • Construction Rebates: Every build commissioned through the FastTrack Tools website yields a 15% $TNB rebate on the total project value.
  • Build Payments: Apply $TNB for a percentage discount on total build prices, or pay for an entire construction project in full!
  • Stock Car League: Participants in the upcoming FastTrack Stock Car League earn $TNB for every event, with winners earning massive multipliers.
  • Asset Buffing (Coming Soon): Add $TNB as a permanent incentive directly to your approved assets to increase their value!

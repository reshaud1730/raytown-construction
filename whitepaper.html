The Trade N Build ($TNB) ecosystem is engineered to revolutionize property development, hardware management, and community engagement within the Upland metaverse. Powered by FastTrack Tools and executed by Raytown Construction, our protocol shifts the power back to the players.

02. The Zero-Fee Philosophy

Upland operations shouldn't be bogged down by recurring overhead. The FastTrack ecosystem operates on a strict Zero-Fee Philosophy.

  • No Software Subscriptions: FastTrack Tools and Ledger Systems are acquired via one-time lifetime licenses or paid fully in $TNB.
  • No Hardware Subscriptions: Operating an RTC Generator requires zero monthly UPX management fees.
  • Ecosystem Fueled: Service maintenance and platform sustainability are funded entirely through the $TNB token velocity and our underlying liquidity pools.

04. RTC Generator Fleet Mechanics

Generators are the backbone of our sub-contracting fleet. Acquiring an official Raytown Construction Generator grants the holder guaranteed yields paid in $TNB. Generators are priced in UPX and come in three distinct tiers.

  • Green Gen: 50,000 UPX (Base Value $25) | 4% Max Monthly Yield
  • Red Gen: 75,000 UPX (Base Value $37.50) | 8% Max Monthly Yield
  • Yellow Gen: 100,000 UPX (Base Value $50) | 12% Max Monthly Yield

Capacity & Yield Triggers: There is no restraint on the amount of Sparklet a user can add to a generator's Power-Up Fuel Tank. However, to achieve maximum "Active Yield," a generator owner must hold at least 1 Active Build assignment. Generators without active assignments will default to their "Passive Yield" rates.

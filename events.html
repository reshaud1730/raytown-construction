🏆 45-Day Tournament Bracket

A "Win & Advance" gauntlet designed for Upland's 6-car grid limit.

Days 1 - 15

Qualifiers: 36 drivers split into 6 random heats. The Top 3 from each heat advance to the semi-finals.

Days 16 - 30

Semi-Finals: 18 drivers split into 3 heats. The Top 2 lock into the Grand Finale. 3rd/4th move to Challengers.

Days 31 - 45

Championship: A Best-of-3 series for the Grand Finale to crown the S1 Champion and lock $TNB multipliers.

## 🛠️ The RTC Reverse SH Formula
We utilize a data-driven approach to track construction progress beyond the standard UI limitations.

### The Equation
$$CSH = R - ((D \times 24 + H) \times S)$$

* **R:** Total Spark Hours Required
* **D/H:** Days and Hours Remaining
* **S:** Current Spark Staked
* **CSH:** Completed Spark Hours (Banked)

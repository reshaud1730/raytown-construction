Technical Brief: Progress Analytics

At Raytown Construction, we eliminate the guesswork from development. While the standard Upland interface provides general estimations, our FastTrack Analytics utilizes a reverse-calculation method to identify banked Completed Spark Hours (CSH) with 100% precision.

Proprietary Reverse SH Equation CSH = R - ((D * 24 + H) * S)

R: Total Project Requirement (Total SH)

S: Current Staked Spark (Decimal)

D / H: Days & Hours Remaining (UI Data)

CSH: Final Banked Spark Hours

The Challenge

Identifying construction progress during variable staking shifts. To maintain the 15% participant reward structure for Trade N Build, we needed exact CSH data to ensure fair distribution of rewards upon completion.

The Application

Requirement (R): 1,200 SH
Remaining: 4 Days, 10 Hours
Current Stake (S): 2.5 Spark

Result: 935.00 CSH Completed

Strategic Outcome: Maximum Efficiency & Automated Transparency

By applying this logic, Raytown Construction reduced project tracking time by 40% and provided 100% transparent data to all project stakeholders in the FastTrack Community Center.
